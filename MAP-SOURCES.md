## Dormitories and rentals

All 18 inherited dorm coordinates and all schematic rental clusters are withheld from the map. Their records remain available for activity tracking; no prayer or visit records were deleted. The original dorm coordinates were generated around inaccurate campus positions, without building addresses or sources. They cannot be corrected by shifting the whole cluster.

The [official KSMU campus page](https://kurskmed.com/department/campus/page/Campus_1) supplies six dorm addresses: Karl Marx 3A, 3B, Yamskaya 16, Karl Marx 69G, Akhtyrskaya 2, Karl Marx 65B. These addresses are recorded, but precise building coordinates still need comparison. The [KSU dormitory document](https://cloud.kursksu.ru/kursksu.ru/pages/2020/March/7/bfQenNBK.pdf) lists dorm 2 at Gogolya 65 and dorm 3 at Kirova 5; it does not support the original dorm 1 record. The [SWSU campus page](https://swsu.ru/structura/ahp/stud_gorod/) uses lettered blocks; the original numbered records cannot safely be matched without further confirmation. Agricultural dorm locations remain unverified.

No evidence was found for the modeled private-renter distribution or schematic room blocks. These are not presented as real surveyed geography. Population and nationality estimates have not been verified by correcting the pins.
